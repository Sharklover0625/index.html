Monthly Bookkeeping

I categorize all transactions, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, and deliver clean monthly financial statements every month — no more guessing where your money went.

QuickBooks Setup & Cleanup

Starting fresh or inheriting a messy QBO file? I'll set up your QuickBooks Online account properly or clean up months of disorganized data so everything is accurate and tax-ready.

Bank Reconciliation

I match every transaction in QuickBooks against your bank statements to ensure your records are 100% accurate and any discrepancies are caught early.
